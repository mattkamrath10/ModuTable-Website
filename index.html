What is MODU/TABLE?

MODU/TABLE is a revolutionary coffee table system designed around interchangeable side panels. Instead of buying a completely new table every time your décor changes, simply slide out one collection and slide in another. Sports. Luxury. Holiday. Collector. Family. Future collections. One frame. Unlimited possibilities.

Engineered To Be Different

Hidden Locking System

A concealed spring-loaded latch secures the tabletop while preventing the interchangeable panels from being removed until intentionally unlocked.

PETG Panels

Durable industrial PETG construction provides lightweight strength while allowing virtually unlimited custom designs.

Universal Frame

Every collection uses the exact same modular frame, allowing complete compatibility across future product lines.
